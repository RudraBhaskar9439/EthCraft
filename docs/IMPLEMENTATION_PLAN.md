## P0 — Confirm scope policies and test foundations

Entry: Local non-token technical setup is authorized. Independent participant proof and funded-chain/token-platform approval remain mandatory in P4/P5 under the user-approved gate migration.

Build:

- Freeze one local server, one plot, one calculator candidate and test manifest, one builder fixture and one bot. Record fixture identities without representing them as independent human participants.
- Keep local work non-token and loopback-only. Pin and physically test a Paper/Java/Mineflayer/Node combination; disclose unsupported historical fixture versions and block production adoption until a maintained route is approved.
- Approve sources of truth, command envelope, lifecycle and role matrices, exact policy boundaries, deployment identities and test environments. Put the operation journal and fault-injection harness in the first mutation implementation.
- Document external prerequisites and owners: authenticated game accounts, 0G provider/funding, Arkiv access, compatible v4 settlement network, RPC, test gas and liquidity. Keep secrets outside the repository and bot context.
- Pre-register E1 to E8 conditions, metrics, held-out scenarios and the pilot/trial selection rule before observing favorable results. Review upstream license and version compatibility before copying or installing source.

Verify:

- The local environment is explicitly non-token and isolated. Actual token-linked platform approval is mandatory in P5 and private/testnet operation is not assumed exempt.
- Version smoke tests cover connection, movement, placement, lever interaction, lamp observation and stock access. Unsupported combinations fail explicitly.
- Define every timeout with units and an equality rule, plus action budgets, maximum payloads, plot bounds, workload limits and recovery targets.
- Approve wallet relinking, ownership after cancellation, exact reward/allocation validity, token decimals and fractional KIT treatment.
- Specify confirmation and deep-reorganization response. A world mutation cannot be undone merely by rolling back a database.
- Choose the inspection lock protocol: keep structure locked while any issued pass remains usable and settlement is pending; early unlocking needs tested onchain invalidation.
- Record every remaining decision with an owner and the phase it blocks. No hidden default may determine custody or payment.

Gate: All 15 local P0 cases pass with evidence and approved engineering review. No independent-human, token-platform or funded-chain check is claimed passed; the transferred P4/P5 cases remain mandatory and blocked until their actual evidence exists.

Stop: A failed local compatibility or policy gate prevents P1. Participant proof blocks P4; actual platform/funded-chain preflight blocks P5 before live token integration. Do not silently substitute fixture identities or assumed balances.

Verify:

## P5 — Establish custody and backed token issuance

Entry: P4 passes. P5-024 resolves actual token-linked platform suitability and P5-025 verifies the chosen chain, deployments, signing roles, gas and liquidity funding before live token/economy setup. No testnet/private exemption is assumed.

Build:

- Complete mandatory token-platform and funded-chain preflight (P5-024/P5-025) before integrated issuance, deployment or market activity; do not infer permission or balances from local tests.
- Lock real complete material bundles in controlled custody. Produce unique deposit receipts and domain-bound issuance authorization.
- Implement controlled demonstration CRED and KIT, exact base-unit arithmetic, issuer roles and atomic used-deposit protection.
- Track item-level backing for circulating KIT and burned-but-undelivered claims. Reconcile custody, supply and delivery obligations before release.

Verify:

- For every item test required quantity minus one, exact and plus one, wrong item identities and removal from reserved custody.
- Generate deposit/issue/transfer/burn/pending/deliver sequences. Ten issued KIT with two burned pending claims still needs ten bundles of backing.
- Replay each deposit concurrently and after restart; alter quantities, recipient and signed domain. Only one authorized issuance can succeed.
- Reject zero/invalid/overflow quantities and unapproved mint roles. A reverting mint must not consume a valid authorization.
- Test declared KIT fractions and whole-unit redemption rules without floating-point rounding; display and chain use identical configured decimals.
- Crash between stock lock, ledger commit and mint tracking. Reorganize issuance logs and restore mismatched snapshots.
- Introduce a one-item reserve deficit, manifest drift and issuer rotation. Stop new issuance while preserving all existing obligations.
- Count pool-held KIT once in total outstanding supply, retain fractional liabilities through integer scaling and keep custody independent from pool depth and prices.

Gate: Every P5 case passes. Every outstanding obligation is backed item by item; unused deposit evidence cannot mint again.

Stop: Any reserve mismatch stops issuance and any purchase flow that cannot safely promise delivery. Burning KIT never clears an undelivered liability.

## P6 — Deploy the state registry hook and final pool

Entry: P5 passes. Verify the selected v4 deployment, PoolManager, router, currencies and test-network liquidity prerequisites.

Build:

- Implement the minimal beforeSwap hook that gates CRED-to-KIT purchases on fresh available state. Validate callback origin and exact pool identity.
- Deploy the hook with correct permission bits and create the final pool with that hook attached. Hook attachment cannot be added retroactively to an existing pool.
- Use compatible official v4 core/periphery interfaces and a supported deployed router. Seed disclosed active ranges; obtain real tick-, fee- and rounding-aware quotes instead of using x*y examples as production pricing.

Verify:

- Test unauthorized/revoked publishers, foreign market, duplicate/lower sequences, out-of-order confirmation and maximum lifetime boundaries.
- Exercise healthy/unavailable crossed with fresh/stale/missing state. Test expiry minus one second, exact expiry and plus one using chain time.
- Swap both currency sort orders, both zeroForOne values and exact-input/exact-output modes. Gate by economic purchase direction.
- Reject foreign PoolManager, pool key, currencies, fee, spacing, registry and malformed hook permissions.
- Use the actual supported router path. Healthy purchases work; stale/unavailable purchases revert; intended sales and liquidity removal remain available.
- Race outage/healthy publication and swaps in both canonical orders. Stop refresh and simulate RPC/gas failure; measure tolerated delay.
- Registry read failure must fail purchases closed. Hook execution never mints, delivers, alters backing or claims control over outside markets.
- Compare active and out-of-range positions, tick crossings, over-cap demand and nominal capital versus executable depth. A thin-pool price cannot alter wages, reputation or solvency.
- Measure pre-report outage exposure, refresh cost and false pauses; disclose that purchase-only gating can shift risky KIT exposure to LPs. Adaptive fees remain outside the initial mechanism.

Gate: Every P6 case passes on the final hooked route. The contract/hook address, registry, pool and router used by P7 are fixed and reviewed.

Stop: No fresh healthy report means no new purchase through this pool. Existing claims and accepted reward claims retain independent paths.

## P7 — Connect bounded purchase redemption and delivery

Entry: P6 passes. Seeded liquidity is disclosed. Durable worker/plugin delivery receipts and confirmation rules are installed.

Build:

- Quote exact whole-KIT output with enforced CRED cap, expiry, chain, route and recipient. The requester signs the bounded purchase; labor escrow is inaccessible.
- Atomically burn whole KIT and create a claim bound to server, job, bundle, quantity and recipient.
- Wait for canonical confirmations, fulfill via the plugin journal, reconcile actual inventory, release only fulfilled reserves and retain pending obligations.

Verify:

- Test cap minus one base unit, exact cap and plus one; quote expiry boundaries, adverse prices, insufficient liquidity, balances, allowance and native gas.
- Tamper with every route/asset/recipient field. Track revert, replacement, drop, delayed confirmation and unknown submission without making a second purchase.
- Force either burn or claim creation to fail; both must revert. Reject fractions, wrong targets, unauthorized spend and insufficient KIT.
- Replay claim logs and semantic claim IDs across concurrent workers, restart and plugin retries. Only confirmed correct-domain evidence authorizes delivery.
- Make destination full, offline, missing or unloaded. Interrupt a multi-item delivery at each journal boundary, including after mutation before receipt.
- Partial/unknown delivery stays pending or quarantined. Previously delivered portions cannot be duplicated and undelivered backing cannot be released.
- Pause purchases or lose Arkiv/publisher service during an existing claim. Preserve claim ownership and deliver when its own safety conditions permit.
- Fail chain acknowledgement after physical delivery; it must not trigger redelivery. Cancellation keeps separate ownership for KIT, claims, unused stock and placed blocks.

Gate: Every P7 case passes. A real KIT purchase produces exactly its backed physical bundle; each retry resolves the original operation without extra charge or fulfillment.

Stop: Unknown transaction or delivery outcome requires reconciliation. Never ask the player to purchase again to fix an existing pending claim.
